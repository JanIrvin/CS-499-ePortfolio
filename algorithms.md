Enhancement Two
The artifact I selected for the algorithms and data structures category is my Treasure Hunt Game that I originally did in CS-370. The project uses deep Q-learning to train a pirate agent to navigate through a maze and find the treasure. I chose this artifact because it shows my experience with algorithms, machine learning, and evaluating computing solutions. For my enhancement, I improved the deep Q-learning training process and added ways I could measure its performance. I used an exploration strategy where epsilon begins at 1.0 and then decreases to 0.05. This lets the pirate explore different actions early in training and rely on what it has learned. I also added tracking the win rate, training loss, number of epochs, and the training time. The enhanced version completed 1,000 epochs in roughly 16.42 minutes and reached a recent win rate of 100%.
This enhancement helped me make progress toward Outcomes 3 and 4 because I used algorithmic principles to improve and evaluate a computing solution. Testing also showed me the importance of using multiple measurements when evaluating an algorithm. Even though the recent win rate reached 100%, the completion check returned false, showing that the model was not consistently solving the maze from every starting position. One challenge I faced was the amount of time required to train and test the model. Through the enhancement, I learned more about the tradeoff between exploration and using the previously learned information. I also learned that a high win rate does not mean an algorithm has completely solved the problem.
